Paragraph 1

Indigenous data sovereignty and knowledge systems

Indigenous data sovereignty is not a technical aspiration · it is the operative condition for Indigenous Peoples to be custodians of the biocultural information of their territories under the CARE principles (Collective benefit · Authority to control · Responsibility · Ethics). The Declaration of the Wise Men and Wise Women at COP16 (Centro Java Liviana · Muysca People · October 2024) states it clearly: "our knowledge systems, given since creation and transmitted from generation to generation, are fundamental to protect Mother Earth". In Colombia, the National Indigenous Organization (ONIC) and the Territorial Monitoring System (SMT) are building the MACI Framework articulating 5 operational modules across Data Council · FPIC protocols · Nagoya ABS · biobank with Indigenous chain of custody · and bioeconomy with fair benefit-sharing. The evidence already exists: the 2024 Catalogue of Useful Plants × Indigenous Reserves geolocates 5,690 species with endorsement from Kew Gardens and GBIF · and Indigenous custodians steward approximately 18 million hectares in NON-Amazonian strategic ecosystems (páramos · Chocó biogeographic hotspot · savannas · drylands) in addition to Amazonian territories. The Brasília Declaration must recognize data sovereignty as a fundamental right complementary to Article 8(j) of the Convention on Biological Diversity (OS8j), and commit to concrete mechanisms so that national Indigenous organizations are recognized as direct fiduciaries without intermediaries that dilute political control.

Paragraph 2

Direct financing without intermediaries

Less than 1% of the USD 1.7 billion committed under the Glasgow Forest Tenure Pledge has reached national Indigenous organizations directly. This architecture is structural · two decades of climate funds channeled through international NGOs have produced fragmented capacity · short projects · and dependency. The Declaration of the Wise Men and Wise Women at COP16 (Centro Java Liviana · Muysca People) states it literally: "concert direct financing strategies with Indigenous Peoples · allowing execution and administration to be carried out directly by Indigenous government structures for the implementation of the Plans of Life". The TRÜA Global Summit on OS8j of the CBD reaffirms this. The Brasília Summit must move beyond declarations of intent toward concrete fiduciary mechanisms: (a) direct accreditations such as GCF DAE for national Indigenous organizations · (b) bilateral country packages with Indigenous title-holding · (c) replicable national territorial funds (Podáali Brazil model) · and (d) philanthropic windows with minimum quotas for direct Indigenous allocation. The commitment must be quantitative · traceable · with deadlines.

Paragraph 3

Biocultural governance and returning to origin

Biocultural governance requires integrating three layers that today operate separately: traditional knowledge · formal science · and sustainable markets. The Declaration of the Wise Men and Wise Women at COP16 issues the underlying political-spiritual call: "to return to origin to change the ambitious, violent, selfish and irresponsible ways of existing" that devastate Mother Earth · and demands recognition of "vital spaces, sacred sites and natural elements · water, mountains, rivers, páramos, seas, savannas, forests and jungles · as living beings, subjects of rights". The MACI Framework translates this mandate into operational architecture: community biocultural inventories with QA/QC and mandatory return of results to communities · genetic capacities and biobank with specific FPIC and Nagoya ABS agreements · incubation of bio-businesses with fair benefit-sharing clauses. Geographic coverage spans Colombia's 5 macroregions with verifiable cases: Matavén certified VCS+CCB · georeferenced Catalogue of Useful Plants · Constitutional Court Ruling U'wa SU-039/1997 · Sierra Nevada Black Line · Chocó Embera. The Brasília Declaration must recognize Indigenous bioeconomy as a legitimate economy of its own · not as a market niche · and commit stable multi-year financial support to integrated programmes such as MACI · not isolated 18-month projects.
