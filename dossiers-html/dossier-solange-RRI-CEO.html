08 · The new component in this briefing

ONIC as methodological steward · RRI coalition

Institutional proposal · replicable south-south

"While other peoples build their own capacities, ONIC safeguards their data"

Sister peoples of the global south (Peru · Bolivia · Brazil · Ecuador · Central America · Africa) need technical capacities they do not yet have — and the RRI coalition can finance the methodological transfer. ONIC proposes to act as temporary methodological steward: hosts the data · trains the local organization · transfers everything once local capacities mature. Model based on the proven experience of Colombia's CNMI sovereign GBV-data custody over 5 years.

10 · CNMI Protocol · proven operational base
CNMI · Sovereign GBV custody · 5 years operating
The replicable protocol we are ALREADY testing in Colombia
The National Commission of Indigenous Women (CNMI) safeguards GBV data with absolute Indigenous sovereignty · 30 indicators in 9 dimensions · 10 without precedent in state systems. Three key protocols transfer to the stewardship model:
  • Distributed cryptographic keys across 7 territorial organizations · no central node can read everything.
  • Council of Women Guardians as ethical authority level N3—N4 · access-request decisions go through plural council.
  • Zero-access sovereignty · neither national ONIC · nor State · nor academia have direct access · only the data-holding people.
