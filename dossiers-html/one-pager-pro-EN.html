Continuity of political mandate

MACI operationally implements the Declaration of Wise Men and Women COP16 (Java Liviana · Muysca People · Oct-2024) and the TRÜA Global Summit recommendations to the OS8j of the Convention on Biological Diversity. "Return to origin · change ambitious, violent, selfish and irresponsible ways of existing" (Wise Ones COP16). Brasília 2026 is the next operational station of this mandate.

Indigenous financial architecture in formulation: dedicated financial mechanism for indigenous peoples (Decree 1088/1993 · MPC) under construction · institutional detail available in bilaterals with invited donors.

Direct support to ongoing initiatives: contributions from USD 100 via SMT-ONIC portal · smt-onic.com/donation/projects/?category=44

What we ask in the room

"Direct fiduciary recognition of ONIC without intermediary vehicles that dilute political control. Less than 1% of post-COP26 USD 1.7B reaches Indigenous national organizations directly. MACI offers the architecture that funders can actually transfer to."
