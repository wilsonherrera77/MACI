05 · Continuity of political mandate

Mandate operationalised

MACI operationalises the Declaration of Sabios y Sabias COP16 (Centro Java Liviana · Muysca People · Oct 2024) and the TRÜA World Summit recommendations to the CBD's OS8j.

To return to origin — to change the ambitious, violent, selfish and irresponsible ways of existing. Declaration of Sabios y Sabias COP16 · Centro Java Liviana · Muysca People · October 2024

Indigenous financial architecture in formulation · dedicated financial mechanism for Indigenous Peoples (Decree 1088/1993 · MPC) under construction · institutional detail available in bilaterals with invited donors.

Direct support to ongoing initiatives · contributions from USD 100 via SMT-ONIC portal · smt-onic.com/donation/projects/?category=44
